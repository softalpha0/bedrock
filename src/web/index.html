Tokenised real-world assets, explained — not just listed.

Bedrock screens the ~7,900 tokenised stocks, treasuries and commodities CoinMarketCap tracks, then goes further than a plain table: it ranks live price spreads between an asset's tokenised wrappers, breaks down who issues them and on which chain, and gives every asset a full research view — company facts, SEC filings, and every backing token.

More than a screener

Finding a price is the easy part. The interesting questions are what backs it, who controls the supply, and whether the number you're looking at is even real.

⌗

Screener & Compare

Filter, search and sort the top 1,000 assets by market cap; check up to 4 to compare side by side, or star ones to watch.

↕

Wrapper spread

The same asset often has several tokenised wrappers from different issuers. Bedrock ranks how far the cheapest and priciest diverge, live.

◔

Issuer & chain share

Who controls tokenised RWA supply, and which blockchain actually hosts it — Ethereum, Solana, Arbitrum and more, chain-neutral by design.

⌘

Terminal

Type a ticker or name, hit enter, get the full research view — no tab-hopping. A dashboard of movers, spreads and new listings sits underneath.

◈

Research view

Every asset links out to its SEC EDGAR filings and its backing tokens' CoinMarketCap pages, plus a plain-English Q&A description.

⇩

CSV export

One click turns any filtered table into a CSV — raw numbers, missing data left blank rather than a misleading zero.

What building this surfaced

Two "wrappers" of the same gold token came back priced ~30x apart — not a bug. One is denominated per gram, the other per troy ounce, and quotes/latest has no unit field to tell them apart. Bedrock filters spreads over 20% out instead of calling them arbitrage. A handful of assets also carry a real price but a literal $0 market cap — shown as missing, not zero.

Built entirely on CoinMarketCap

No other market-data source. The API key never reaches the browser — a small server holds it and proxies an allow-list of endpoints.
